2. What data we process

This site has no forms and does not create user profiles. We only process the data you give us when you email us, message us on WhatsApp or call us: usually your name, phone number, email and details about the car you are interested in.

Browsing also generates technical data such as your IP address or browser type. If you accept analytics cookies, usage statistics are collected too. See the cookie policy for details.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiries and arrange viewings. The legal basis is your consent when contacting us and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To manage the sale if you buy a car. The legal basis is the performance of the contract.
  • To analyse how the site is used and improve it, only if you accept analytics cookies. The legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and accounting. The legal basis is compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not send you marketing without your express consent.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as the conversation lasts and up to one year afterwards, unless it leads to a purchase. In that case we keep it for the periods required by law.

5. Who we share it with

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. Some providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service: web hosting, email and, if you message us on WhatsApp, Meta Platforms. The latter may involve international transfers covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
