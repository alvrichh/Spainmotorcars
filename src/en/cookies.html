Cookie policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In accordance with article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and the GDPR, this page explains which cookies this website uses and how you can manage them. This is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They make the site work, remember your preferences or provide usage statistics.

2. What this site uses

  • Technical and preference storage. We store your cookie choice and your light or dark mode preference in your browser. It does not identify you and does not require consent.
  • Analytics. We do not currently use analytics cookies. If we add them, they will only run if you accept them in the notice, and we will list them here.
  • Third parties. None. The video, images and fonts are served from our own domain.

4. Accepting, rejecting or changing your choice

On your first visit we show a notice so you can accept or reject non-essential cookies. Rejecting them does not affect browsing.

To change your choice, clear the cookies and site data in your browser. The notice will appear again on your next visit.

6. International transfers
